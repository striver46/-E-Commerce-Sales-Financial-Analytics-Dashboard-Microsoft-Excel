## 🎯 Project Overview

Ek **end-to-end Excel dashboard** jo e-commerce sales data ko analyze karke business ke liye actionable insights deta hai: revenue trends, product performance, customer segments aur delivery/customer-satisfaction metrics, sab ek hi interactive screen par.

**Business questions jinka answer dashboard deta hai:**

- 💰 Kitna revenue aur profit generate hua, aur trend kya hai?
- 🌍 Kaun se geographic regions sabse zyada revenue la rahe hain?
- 📦 Kaun si product categories aur bestsellers business ko drive kar rahe hain?
- 🛒 Kaun sa sales channel / customer type best perform kar raha hai?
- ⭐ Customers kitne satisfied hain aur delivery kitni fast hai?

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🌎 Geographic Concentration

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/geo_share.png" alt="Revenue by Geography" width="90%">
</p>

- 🥇 **Los Angeles** sabse bada contributor: **33.2%** of total revenue
- 🥈 **San Diego** second: **15.2%**
- 🎯 Dono cities milkar **~48%** revenue deti hain, yaani revenue kaafi concentrated hai. Isse targeted marketing aur inventory planning ka scope milta hai.

### 📉 Sales Trends, Products & Customers

- Dashboard **13 weeks** ke sales trend ko track karta hai (revenue vs gross profit)
- **Product performance** category-wise aur Top 5 bestsellers ke saath visible hai
- **Customer segments** (Business / New / Returning) aur **sales channels** ke hisaab se slice karna possible hai
- Customer satisfaction average **3.96/5** aur delivery average **2.34 days**, jo healthy fulfilment performance dikhata hai

## ✨ Dashboard Features

| Feature | Description |
|:--|:--|
| 🎛️ **Interactive Slicers** | Order Date timeline, Customer Type, Region, Sales Channel |
| 📊 **Revenue & Gross Profit Trend** | Smooth line chart with data labels |
| 🏷️ **KPI Cards** | Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Avg Price, Units Sold |
| 🗺️ **Revenue by Country** | Horizontal bar chart for geographic comparison |
| 🍩 **Revenue by Channel** | Donut chart showing channel mix |
| 🛍️ **Product Category & Top 5 Bestsellers** | Identify winning products quickly |
| 🔗 **Navigation Buttons** | Analyze / Data / Contact sheets ke liye quick links |
